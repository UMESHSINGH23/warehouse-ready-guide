Warehouse Operations of Alcohol

Follow these six steps in order to complete your tasks in the Alcohol Warehouse section safely and efficiently.

LESSON
!
NON-NEGOTIABLE

If a task feels unsafe, stop and ask.

Never guess how to use equipment, enter restricted areas or handle an unknown hazard. Follow site training, posted signs and your supervisor's instructions.

01

Use required PPE

Wear the protective equipment required for your work area and check that it fits properly.

02

Keep routes clear

Do not block walkways, emergency exits, fire equipment or marked vehicle routes.

03

Report hazards

Report spills, damaged goods, unsafe equipment and near misses promptly.

04

Don't bypass training

Do not operate moving Vehicle unless trained and authorised by your employer.
