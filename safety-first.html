THE ARRIVAL ROUTINE

Four checks before your shift

Your employer's local instructions always take priority. The methods shown here are illustrative only.

01
🦺

Wear your PPE

Put on the required high-visibility orange vest and safety boots. Check that they fit and are in good condition.

02
📝

Written attendance

If your site requires a sign-in sheet, enter your own details accurately and follow the posted instructions. Never sign for someone else.

03
▦

Scan your badge

If your site uses an attendance terminal, scan your own badge and wait for confirmation. Do not share badges or bypass the system.

04
🚪

Enter when authorised

Follow access controls, pedestrian routes and your supervisor's directions. Ask if your attendance does not register or you are unsure where to go.

!

Important: this is a practice simulation

The attendance sheet and badge scanner are visual examples only. This page does not record attendance, scan a real badge or replace your workplace induction. Use only the process approved at your warehouse.
